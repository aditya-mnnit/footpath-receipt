    A little transparency

    Notes in, draft out.

    Footpath Receipt sends your typed notes and optional typed area label to Google’s hosted Gemini API, where the open-weight Gemma 4 model organizes them. The API key stays on the local server, not in browser code.

    Gemma must attach each item to a note ID and an exact quote. The server checks that the quote occurs in the cited note. This verifies provenance, not truth. The model cannot see your block, verify its state, judge safety or accessibility, or contact anyone.
